Support — Follow Money

Need help? Email support.follow.money@gmail.com or use Settings → Submit a Ticket in the app. We usually reply within a few days.

How do I delete my account?

Open Settings → Account, tap ⋯ in the top-right corner and choose Delete Account. You'll be asked to sign in with Apple or Google once more to confirm. This permanently deletes your account and all of its data from our servers and your device. If you signed in with Apple, the app is also removed from your Apple ID's "Sign in with Apple" list.

To remove your entries but keep the account, choose Erase All My Data in the same menu instead.

How do I export my data?

Open the Reports tab and use the export menu. You can export to CSV, Excel or PDF, or as a ZIP file that includes your receipt photos.

Are my receipt photos uploaded?

No. Receipt photos stay on your device. Receipts are read on the device, and only the recognized text is synced with your entries.

My Apple Pay payments aren't being added

The app adds Wallet payments through a Shortcuts automation. Open Settings → Apple Pay Automation in the app and follow the setup steps. The Shortcuts "Transaction" trigger does not fire for every purchase: some in-app and online payments never start it, so add those manually.

How do I recover a deleted entry?

Deleted entries stay in Settings → Recent Actions for 30 days.
