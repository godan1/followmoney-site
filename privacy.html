Privacy Policy — Follow Money

Last updated: November 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Follow Money app ("the App") collects, uses, and protects your information.

1. Data We Collect

To provide the App's core functionality — syncing your records between your devices — the App stores the following information on our servers:

  • Account details from Sign in with Apple or Google Sign-In: an account identifier, and the email address and name that the provider shares with the App (Sign in with Apple lets you hide your email address)
  • Transaction entries you create, import, scan or log through the Apple Pay automation: amount, currency, type, category, merchant name, date, notes, VAT, payment method, card name, and the text recognized from a scanned receipt
  • Custom categories you create

All of this data is linked to your account and used only to provide the App's features. We do not use analytics or advertising SDKs, do not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and do not show ads.

2. Data That Stays on Your Device

  • Receipt photos and scans are stored only on your device. They are never uploaded to our servers; only the text recognized from them is synced.
  • Receipt reading runs entirely on your device, using Apple's text recognition and, where available, Apple Intelligence. Receipt contents are not sent to us or to any AI service.
  • App preferences (currency, language, theme), saved CSV column mappings, merchant category rules, and the log of Apple Pay automation calls stay on your device.

3. Where Your Data Is Stored

  • Synced data is stored on Firebase (Google Cloud) servers, associated with your account.
  • Data in transit and at rest is encrypted using the security measures provided by Firebase.
  • Access is restricted by security rules so that only your signed-in account can read or change your data.
  • We do not sell your data. Firebase acts as our data processor and does not use your data for its own purposes beyond providing the underlying infrastructure, per Google's Firebase data processing terms.

4. Permissions the App Requests

  • Camera — used only when you choose to scan or photograph a receipt.
  • Photos — the App uses the system photo picker, which gives it only the photos you select. It does not request access to your photo library.

5. Third-Party Services

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes. The App relies on:

  • Firebase/Google Cloud, as our infrastructure provider, to store and sync your data
  • Apple and Google, for account sign-in and, if you purchase a paid feature, for processing that payment
  • Public currency-rate services (frankfurter.dev, with jsDelivr as a fallback), from which the App downloads exchange rates. These requests contain only currency codes and dates, never your entries.

If you set up the Apple Pay automation in the Shortcuts app, iOS passes the amount, merchant and card name of a Wallet payment to the App, which saves it as an entry. You can turn the automation off in Shortcuts at any time.

6. Paid Features

The App may offer optional paid features or subscriptions in the future (for example, additional export formats, advanced reports, or increased storage). Payments are processed through Apple's App Store — we do not directly receive or store your payment card details.

7. Your Control Over Your Data

  • Edit or delete any entry or category at any time. Deleted entries are kept for 30 days in Settings → Recent Actions so you can recover accidental deletions, then removed permanently.
  • Export your entries at any time from the Reports tab (CSV, Excel, PDF, or a ZIP with receipt photos).
  • Erase All My Data (Settings → Account → ⋯) deletes every entry and category from our servers and your device but keeps your account.
  • Delete Account (Settings → Account → ⋯) permanently deletes your account and all associated data from our servers and your device. If you signed in with Apple, it also revokes the App's access to your Apple ID, so the App disappears from your Apple ID's "Sign in with Apple" list.

8. Data Retention

We keep your synced data for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, its data is removed from our servers immediately.

9. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children.

10. Development Disclosure

This App was developed with the assistance of AI tools (including AI-assisted coding and design). AI tools were used to help write and refine the application's code and design; your transaction data is not shared with these AI tools as part of the App's normal operation.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.

12. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, email us at support.follow.money@gmail.com or use "Submit a Ticket" in Settings within the App.
